# BUD v0.7 — Management Reporting Data Dictionary

**Status:** Portfolio prototype; fictional records; no live ERP integration.

| Field | Definition | Grain / rule |
|---|---|---|
| Project ID (ERP) | Official consecutive ERP project identifier | Unique project master key; never generated by Excel |
| Customer | Customer identifier | Project |
| Business Unit | Strategic Services or Brands | Project; Automation and Machining out of scope |
| Brand | Manufacturer / commercial brand | Quotation and project; N/A for Strategic Services |
| Selling Entity | Company issuing the customer invoice: MX or USA | Project and reporting activity |
| Sale Currency | Original commercial-document currency | Preserve original; prototype reporting uses USD |
| Reporting Month | YYYY-MM | Project-month |
| Customer PO Intake USD | New authorized customer orders and net approved revisions effective in the period | Flow; count each order or revision once |
| Invoiced This Month USD | Customer invoice amount issued during reporting period | Flow |
| Investment 1 — Paid USD | Supplier disbursements paid in the period | Flow |
| Investment 2 — Open USD EOM | Unpaid exposure outstanding at period end | Snapshot; never sum balances across months |
| ERP Recognized Cost USD | ERP-recognized project costs for the period | Flow; may overlap paid disbursements |
| Invoice–Cost Gap USD | Period invoicing less period ERP-recognized cost | Timing-sensitive indicator; not definitive project profit |
| Source Reference | Expected ERP extract and timestamp | Data lineage; fictional in prototype |

## Reporting design
The historical project master is separate from monthly project activity. A project can appear in multiple reporting months. Management reporting can be grouped by **business unit**, **brand**, and **selling entity** without duplicating transactions.

## Commercial and intercompany controls
- One brand per quotation; retain the customer PO reference if a customer combines multiple quotations.
- Prevent double counting of a customer PO and its net revisions in monthly order intake.
- For a Mexico customer sale sourced via the USA entity, attribute the MX project's cost to the MX-side ERP purchase/import records. Do not add the US external supplier purchase to that same MX project again.
- Investment 1, Investment 2, and recognized cost are distinct financial perspectives; do not add them to derive total project cost.
- No statutory consolidation, tax, general ledger accounting or intercompany elimination is performed.

## ERP mapping required for production
Project ID, customer, BU, brand, selling entity, original currency, authorized PO and amendment dates, invoice date/amount, supplier payments, month-end outstanding exposure, ERP cost recognition date/amount, extraction timestamp, and source reference.

## Data-quality checks for a future implementation
1. Unique Project ID in project master; one row per project-month in summarized monthly activity.
2. All activity rows map to a known Project ID and valid BU / brand / selling entity.
3. Customer PO intake and revisions are not counted more than once.
4. Month-end outstanding balances are not accumulated across periods.
5. MX + USA totals reconcile to the management-report total; in-scope business units reconcile likewise.
6. A documented FX policy is required before importing mixed-currency real transactions.

**Validation boundary:** The workbook's sample figures are illustrative; no real ERP reconciliation or live automation is claimed.


## Scope clarification — 16 October 2026
The BUD administrative reporting boundary begins with receipt of an approved customer PO. Customer PO authorized value plus approved amendments is the project commercial baseline. Sales-owned pre-award quotation/pricing/markup/margin fields must not feed BUD management KPIs. Keep authorized PO value, cumulative invoicing, unpaid exposure, paid investment and ERP-recognized costs separately defined; do not treat PO value as realized revenue or sum overlapping cost/payment measures. The current published v0.7 PO-aligned workbook implements the administrative scope in its 20-sheet prototype; the original 21-sheet version remains historical. Documentary PO amendment approval remains unverified.
