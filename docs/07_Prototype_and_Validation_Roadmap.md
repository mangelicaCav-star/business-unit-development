# Prototype and Validation Roadmap

1. Historical reconstruction (current).
2. Proposed simulated Excel prototype: projects, customer PO revisions, budgets, supplier POs, receipts, deliveries, invoices, costs and monthly reporting.
3. Fictional tests: standard purchase, cross-border costing, scope change, partial receipt, supplier delay, disputed ERP cost, US invoice confirmation.
4. Validate formulas, cross-sheet IDs, statuses and reconciliations.
5. Publish test evidence and limitations.

**No prototype has yet been implemented or validated.**
