# Business Mandate and Scope

Operational Management initiated a Strategic Services unit after existing industrial customers requested third-party purchasing/intermediation. The administrator was assigned to make it operational without a predefined operating model.

## Contribution
Developed costing and project budget templates, sourcing and procurement handoffs, logistics coordination, customer communications, invoicing follow-up and project cost reconciliation.

## Existing resources
Sales, Purchasing, Warehouse, Invoicing, Finance, Engineering, ERP, Mexico and US legal entities and cross-border infrastructure predated the unit.

## Attribution
The administrator did not originate the business initiative, personally issue supplier POs, book receipts, invoice customers, edit accounting entries or manage Engineering. Distribution brands existed before the new unit.

## Case question
How can a management-sponsored opportunity become a repeatable, traceable operation using existing corporate resources?
