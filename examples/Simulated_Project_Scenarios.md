# Simulated Project Scenarios

All scenarios are fictional and intended for future validation.

- **SIM-001:** Standard customer PO, official budget, supplier PO, receipt, delivery, invoice, project close and cost reconciliation.
- **SIM-002:** Customer PO changes from 10 to 12 units; preserve original budget, issue new version and separate supplier PO for 2 additional units under same project.
- **SIM-003:** 20 units ordered; 12 received and delivered; invoice only 12.
- **SIM-004:** Incorrect ERP project charge remains annotated until originating department corrects it.
- **SIM-005:** Cost 1,000, markup 30%, price 1,300, gross margin on sales 23.08%.
